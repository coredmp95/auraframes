| test/library caller → httpx transport | The new `transport` param lets a caller choose the network I/O layer. It is only reachable from code that constructs `Client`/`Aura` (library callers and tests), not from any remote input. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-01 | Tampering | `Client.__init__` transport param | low | accept | Param defaults to `None`; the real HTTPTransport is built exactly as before when unset. No remote/untrusted caller can reach the constructor — injection is compile-time/library-level only. Additive-only change introduces no new production attack surface. |
| T-04-02 | Elevation of Privilege | `Aura.__init__` client param | low | accept | Injected `Client` carries no elevated capability beyond what `Aura()` already builds itself; auth/login mechanism is untouched (deferred to a later architecture-review candidate). |
